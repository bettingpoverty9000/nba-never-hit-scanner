How the ranking works

It first identifies real player-stat contracts, then sorts candidates using the market's available price, spread, and trading volume. It does not call a market a statistical edge just because a player has never hit a line. Historical game logs, confirmed minutes, injuries, matchup and schedule context still need independent verification before a bet.

A strong-looking price is not automatically value. Compare the implied probability with a defensible player-performance estimate, and account for liquidity and fees.
